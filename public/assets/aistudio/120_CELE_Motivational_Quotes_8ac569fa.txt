CELE REMINDER
You do not need to master everything in one day. Solve problems consistently, review your mistakes,
strengthen your fundamentals, and keep showing up. Every practice problem is a step toward becoming
a competent civil engineer.

Note: This is a motivational compilation, not a verified scholarly quotation reference. Some popular
sayings have uncertain or disputed attributions.
