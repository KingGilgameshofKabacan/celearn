[PSAD]
DEVELOPMENT LENGTH:
• The minimum embedment length of a reinforcing bar required to develop its design yield strength without pullout failure in concrete.
• Governed by NSCP 2015 Section 425 and ACI 318 provisions.
• Dependent on bar diameter, clear concrete cover, bar spacing, and concrete compressive strength f'c.

• PLASTIC HINGE: A localized yielding zone in a structural flexural member where large inelastic rotations take place under a constant plastic moment Mp.

LATERAL-TORSIONAL BUCKLING:
- An out-of-plane instability failure mode of unbraced beams in flexure.
- Occurs when the compression flange buckles sideways while the cross-section twists.
- Prevented when unbraced length Lb is kept below limiting length Lp (AISC 360 / NSCP 2015).

MODULUS OF RUPTURE:
• The theoretical flexural tensile stress at which an unreinforced concrete beam experiences first cracking.
• Formulated under NSCP 2015 Section 419.2.3 as fr = 0.62 · λ · √f'c (in MPa for normal-weight concrete).

- PUNCHING SHEAR: Critical two-way shear failure occurring around column perimeters in footings and flat slabs, evaluated at distance d/2 from the column face.

[HPGE]
WATER HAMMER:
• A destructive hydraulic acoustic shock wave created in closed pipes when fluid flow velocity changes abruptly due to rapid valve closure.
• Pressure surge is calculated by the Joukowsky equation: ΔP = ρ · c · Δv.
• Prevented using surge tanks, air chambers, slow-closing motorized valves, and surge relief valves.

• HYDRAULIC JUMP: A rapid transition in open channel flow where high-velocity supercritical flow (Fr > 1) abruptly changes into low-velocity subcritical flow (Fr < 1) with large energy dissipation.

VOID RATIO:
- The fundamental soil phase ratio of the volume of void spaces (Vv) to the volume of solid soil particles (Vs): e = Vv / Vs.
- Unlike porosity which is strictly less than 1.0, void ratio can exceed 1.0 in soft organic soils and clays.

QUICK CONDITION:
• A boiling condition in saturated cohesionless soils where upward seepage drag forces equal the submerged soil unit weight, causing effective stress to drop to zero.
• Occurs at the critical hydraulic gradient icr = (Gs - 1) / (1 + e).

- METACENTRIC HEIGHT: The distance between the center of gravity (G) and metacenter (M) of a floating vessel, serving as the primary index of initial static stability.

[MSTE]
STOPPING SIGHT DISTANCE:
• The minimum roadway distance required by a motor vehicle driver traveling at design speed to perceive an unexpected stationary road hazard and bring the vehicle to a complete safe stop.
• Formulated as SSD = 0.278 · V · t + V² / [254 · (f ± G)].
• Accounts for a standard 2.5-second driver perception-reaction time and longitudinal tire-pavement braking friction.

• FREE FLOAT: In a CPM schedule network, the amount of time an activity can be delayed without delaying the early start date of any immediately succeeding activity.

GREENSHIELDS MODEL:
- The classic macroscopic traffic stream model postulating a linear relationship between vehicle speed and traffic density: v = vf · (1 - k/kj).
- Yields a parabolic flow-density relationship where maximum roadway capacity occurs at exactly half free-flow speed and half jam density.

ECONOMIC LIMIT OF HAUL:
• The total haul distance where the financial cost of hauling excavated cut equals the cost of borrowing fill from a nearby borrow pit plus excavation waste.
• Formulated as ELH = Free Haul Distance + (Cost of Borrow / Cost of Overhaul).

- CAPITALIZED COST: The present worth of an engineering asset or public works infrastructure project that is assumed to provide service perpetually, including initial construction and perpetual replacements.
